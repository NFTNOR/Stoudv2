01

Principles

Precision over decoration

Every element earns its place. Remove before adding. White space is not emptiness — it is confidence.

The circle as identity

The circle recurs across all surfaces — the seal, transitions, cursor, menu. It is not decoration. It is structure.

Motion with meaning

Animation is used to guide, reveal, and orient — never to entertain. If removing it breaks the experience, it was wrong.

Monochrome first

The palette is ink on paper. Colour contrast carries meaning. When in doubt: black, white, and the spaces between.

Responsive at its core

All sizing uses clamp(). Nothing breaks. Layouts adapt without breakpoint hacks.

Accessible by default

All colours meet WCAG AA. Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion. Skip links and focus states are never suppressed.

06

The Circle

The circle is not decoration — it is the brand's structural DNA. It appears in seven distinct contexts, each carrying the same idea: precision, completeness, orbit. A mark that closes on itself.

Background Rings

Two concentric rings breathe slowly behind all content. They ground the page without distracting from it.

Every page · fixed · z-index 0

The Seal

The «.» after STOUD is a live seal — an orbiting moon and rotating text ring that activate on the home page.

Home · SVG animation · on load

Nav Dot

An 8–9px filled circle scales in from zero on the active page and on hover. The brand mark at micro scale.

All pages · nav · active / hover state

Cursor

The system cursor is replaced by the same 8px dot. Scales to 14px on interactive elements. Hidden on touch devices.

All pages · pointer · desktop only
→

Page Transitions

Navigation between pages reveals the new page via an expanding circle rooted at the STOUD logo. Cross-document View Transitions API.

All pages · clip-path:circle() · 0.62s

Mobile Menu

A large circle fills the menu's upper area — links live inside it. Opens from the MENU button position.

Subpages · mobile · ::before pseudo-element

07

Layout Grid

All subpages use a two-column flex layout: content (max 690px, flex-grow) + nav-col (fixed ~190px min-width). Below 860px, the nav-col is hidden and replaced by the mobile bar.

Content · max-width: 690px · flex: 1 1 auto
Nav col · min-width: 190px
gap: clamp(40px, 6vw, 120px) · max-width: 1560px · margin: 0 auto

The home page uses a different layout: a centred .stage with a .composition flex container — wordmark left, panel (menu) right, with parallax tilt on mouse move.

08

Voice & Tone

STOUD writes with authority, not volume. Short sentences. No buzzwords. Present tense. The period at the end of STOUD. is not punctuation — it is a statement.

Avoid We are a leading, innovative, full-service law firm offering best-in-class solutions.
Use STOUD sets the new standard for intellectual property law in Norway.
Avoid Our team of experienced professionals are here to help you navigate complex legal landscapes.
Use We quietly lead, giving our clients the assurance that they are always one step ahead.
Avoid Click here to get in touch with us today!
Use You are welcome to get in touch in confidence at info@stoud.com

Headline style

Page titles use one word + period when possible: About. · People. · Expertise. — or a short phrase: Work at STOUD.
The period is part of the identity. It closes the thought. It signals precision.
